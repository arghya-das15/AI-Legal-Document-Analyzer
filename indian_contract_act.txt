INDIAN CONTRACT ACT, 1872

SECTION 10 - WHAT AGREEMENTS ARE CONTRACTS
An agreement becomes a contract when it satisfies the legal requirements of the Act, including lawful consideration and lawful object, and is not expressly declared void.

SECTION 11 - WHO ARE COMPETENT TO CONTRACT
A person must satisfy the legal requirements of competency to contract under the Act.

SECTION 13 - CONSENT
Two or more persons consent when they agree upon the same thing in the same sense.

SECTION 14 - FREE CONSENT
Consent is free when it is not caused by coercion, undue influence, fraud, misrepresentation, or mistake, subject to the Act.

SECTION 15 - COERCION
Coercion concerns acts or threats used to obtain consent, as defined by the Act.

SECTION 16 - UNDUE INFLUENCE
Undue influence concerns situations where one party is in a position to dominate the will of another and uses that position to obtain an unfair advantage.

SECTION 17 - FRAUD
Fraud covers intentional deception in relation to an agreement, as defined by the Act.

SECTION 18 - MISREPRESENTATION
Misrepresentation concerns certain false statements or conduct that cause another person to enter into an agreement.

SECTION 19 - AGREEMENTS WITHOUT FREE CONSENT
When consent is caused by coercion, fraud, or misrepresentation, the agreement may be affected in the manner provided by the Act.

SECTION 23 - LAWFUL CONSIDERATION AND OBJECT
The consideration and object of an agreement must satisfy the statutory requirements of lawfulness. Certain prohibited agreements may be void.

SECTION 27 - AGREEMENT IN RESTRAINT OF TRADE
Agreements restraining a person from exercising a lawful profession, trade, or business are addressed by this section, subject to statutory exceptions.

SECTION 73 - COMPENSATION FOR LOSS OR DAMAGE CAUSED BY BREACH
A party suffering from breach of contract may be entitled to compensation for loss or damage arising in the manner provided by the Act.

SECTION 74 - COMPENSATION WHERE PENALTY IS STIPULATED
Where a contract specifies a sum payable on breach or contains another stipulation by way of penalty, reasonable compensation may be available subject to the statutory requirements.

SECTION 75 - PARTY RIGHTFULLY RESCINDING CONTRACT
A party who rightfully rescinds a contract may be entitled to compensation in accordance with the Act.
